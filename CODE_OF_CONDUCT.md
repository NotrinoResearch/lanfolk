# Code of Conduct

Lanfolk is a small project for people who want to play games together. We want
it to be a friendly, safe place to ask questions, report problems and send
changes, whatever your background, experience, language or identity.

## Our standards

Do:

- be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and with people who are
  writing in a language that is not their first;
- give and accept feedback on the work, not on the person;
- assume good faith, and ask when you are not sure what someone meant;
- keep discussions on topic and in English, so that everybody can follow.

Do not:

- insult, harass, threaten or demean anyone;
- discriminate against anyone for any personal characteristic;
- post private information about others (addresses, friend codes, real names,
  messages) without their permission;
- use sexualized language or images, or give unwelcome attention;
- ask for or share cheats, pirated software or help to break a game's rules
  or terms of service;
- keep going after someone has asked you to stop.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull
requests, discussions and any place where you represent the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.
They may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that
break this code, and may warn, temporarily block or permanently ban a person
for behavior they judge inappropriate, threatening or harmful. They will
explain their decision when it is safe to do so.

To report a problem, use GitHub's private reporting on the repository
(**Security** tab, **Report a vulnerability**) and start the title with
"Conduct:", or contact a maintainer privately through their GitHub profile.
Reports are handled in confidence. Maintainers who are involved in a report
step aside from deciding it.

## What needs a commercial license

Any use for a commercial purpose. Some examples:

- A company using Lanfolk for its work or its staff.
- An internet café, LAN center or gaming venue running it for customers.
- Selling Lanfolk, bundling it with a product, or offering it as a service.
- Building a commercial product on its code.

If you are not sure whether your use counts as commercial, ask before you
start.
